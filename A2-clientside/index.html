Our mission

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and thrive. Bright Futures Foundation is a not-for-profit organisation that raises funds and awareness for education and wellbeing programs in regional communities.

Our events are hosted by volunteers and supported by local businesses. Whether you run, walk, dine or bid, you are part of something bigger than the event itself — you are helping a young person build a brighter future.
